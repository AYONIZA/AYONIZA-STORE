Last updated: September 2026

02 How we use your information

  • To process and confirm your orders, sent to us via WhatsApp.

Account sign-in is handled securely by Firebase Authentication (Google). We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. Your details are only used to fulfil your order and communicate with you about it.
